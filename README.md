# Skillup--AI-

SkillUpAI is a career development platform designed to help students and job seekers 
identify suitable career paths, create professional resumes, and receive job suggestions 
based on their skills and interests. The system integrates career guidance, resume building, 
andinterview preparation , easy-to-use platform. This project aims to simplify career 
planning, and suitable job roles as that align with their skills and goals.

Problem Definition 
Students and freshers often lack guidance in choosing the right career roles, preparing 
professional resumes, and practicing for interviews. Identifying roles that fit their skills 
and preparing for role-specific interviews can be challenging. SkillUpAI addresses these 
issues by offering AI-driven career role suggestions, interview question generation, and 
resume building tools.
Objectives of the Project 
- Guide users in identifying suitable career roles using AI 
- Provide personalized interview questions based on chosen roles
